Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 12, 2026
Last Updated: March 12, 2026

Welcome to EatSnap AI. This Privacy Policy explains how Wenlong Wu ("we," "us," or "our") protects your privacy in accordance with Apple's App Store Guidelines and global data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Body Metrics: Gender, age, height, weight, and activity level used for personalized nutritional calculations.
  • Dietary Records: Food photos and text descriptions analyzed by our AI to estimate calories and nutrients.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Identifiers: Standard identifiers (Device Model, OS version) are used to ensure app stability and manage subscription status.

2. Third-Party Service Providers (SDKs)

To provide our services, we share limited, encrypted data with the following trusted partners:

  • RevenueCat: To manage in-app subscriptions and verify your Pro membership status.
  • Google Firebase: For anonymous app usage statistics and crash reporting to improve stability.
  • Alibaba Cloud (DashScope): Our core AI engine. Food images and text are transmitted via secure TLS 1.3 encryption for real-time nutritional analysis.

3. AI Data Protection & Privacy

  • No-Training Guarantee: We access AI models via professional API channels. Your photos and dietary data are NEVER used to train, retrain, or improve general AI models.
  • No Commercial Sale: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties for marketing or advertising.

4. Data Retention & International Transfer

  • Temporary AI Processing: Food photos sent for AI analysis are processed in transient memory and are not stored permanently on our AI servers.
  • Local & Cloud Storage: Your records are stored locally on your device and within your private iCloud. We do not maintain a central database of your personal photos.
  • International Transfer: As a global service, your data may be processed on secure servers located in various regions where our service providers operate. All transfers are protected by industry-standard encryption.

5. Your Rights & Control

  • Right to Access/Delete: You can view your history at any time or use the "Clear Diet History" button in Settings to permanently delete all nutritional records from your device and iCloud.
  • Right to Limit Processing: You can choose to log food via text only to avoid sharing photos.

THIS APP IS NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE AND DOES NOT PROVIDE PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL ADVICE, DIAGNOSIS, OR TREATMENT. ALWAYS CONSULT A HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL BEFORE STARTING A NEW NUTRITION PLAN.
